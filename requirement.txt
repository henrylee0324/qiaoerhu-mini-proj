quirements : Your project should include the following:
● Develop a digital business idea that creates value primarily through digital technologies.
● Design a business model using key elements from both the Value Proposition Canvas and the Business Model Canvas.
● Use AI to refine and strengthen your business concept, including market positioning, customer value proposition, and business strategy.
● Use AI to improve your presentation materials, including content organization, visual design, graphics, and storytelling.
● Use AI to create mockups or prototypes of your business, such as user interfaces, websites, mobile apps, product concepts, marketing
materials, or customer journeys.
Students are encouraged to experiment with multiple AI tools and demonstrate how AI contributed to the development of their project. Be reminded that
AI is a tool for enhancing your work—not replacing your own judgment.
Evaluation Criteria : Projects will be evaluated based on:
● Innovation and originality of the business idea
● Completeness and coherence of the business model
● Effective integration of digital technologies
● Quality of AI-assisted ideation, refinement, and prototyping
● Professionalism and clarity of the presentation
● Evidence of genuine effort and critical thinking, rather than relying solely on AI-generated content
